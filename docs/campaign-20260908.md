# Five-level campaign implementation

Approved by the owner after the five-level proposal. This expands the three-stage visual direction into five playable levels while preserving its mechanical -> infected -> alien progression. Ship/shop redesign remains deferred to the owner's joint visual review.

| Level | Waves | New encounter | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orbital Foundry |1–5| Mechanical drones/sentries |Battleship|
| Infected Salvage |6–10| Lurkers/crawlers amid machinery |Assault Mech|
| Spore Wilds |11–15| Seeders and gliding skimmers |Seed Matriarch|
| Living Labyrinth |16–20| Needle creatures and flanking crawlers |Tendril Warden|
| Brood Heart |21–25| Colony masses and mixed alien attacks |Brood Mother|

Wilds and Labyrinth currently use distinct cached procedural scenery; new creatures/bosses use readable procedural silhouettes. These are staging visuals, not the final painted redesign. The Labyrinth name does not imply terrain collision: actual split routes and Brood Lattice interactive structures remain for the planned living-world phase.

Victory follows the final salvage sweep. Optional harder replay starts a new five-level campaign, raising enemy HP 25% per loop before rounding; no carried loadout or new save schema. Normal Launch starts standard difficulty. Existing core/economy changes from Claude's review are retained.
