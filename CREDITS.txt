SPORE WARS — a 640K Games production
====================================

AI-generated content disclosure
-------------------------------
Some illustrations are AI-assisted: generated with OpenAI image generation under 640K Games' art
direction, then edited, encoded and composed by 640K Games. Each is listed individually below
(the three world backdrops, the Mothership, Mech and Battleship bodies, the crawler and lurker
bodies, the title hangar and the salvage trader). Everything else - sprites derived from the
credited packs, the studio logo, procedural art, code and sound synthesis - is human-made.
Declare this on store pages that ask (itch.io has an AI-generation disclosure field).
